Show Command Request Catcher — Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026

Show Command Request Catcher (the "extension") helps live sellers on Whatnot capture product requests from their live-show chat and organize them into a queue for their team. This policy explains exactly what data the extension handles.

In short: the extension keeps your data on your own devices and servers. It sends nothing to the developer, shows no ads, and does no tracking, profiling, or selling of data.

What the extension accesses

  • Whatnot live-chat content. On whatnot.com pages you have open, the extension reads chat messages (the sender's username and message text) in order to detect messages that contain your configured request trigger (for example #). This runs locally in your browser.
  • Your queue and settings. Detected requests and your extension settings are stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage.
  • Data you choose to send to your own server. If you enter a team-server address and key in the extension's settings, the extension sends request items — the requester's username, the request text, timestamps, and any price/winner you enter — to that server, which you operate (your Show Command app). The developer neither receives nor has access to this data.
  • Account sign-in. The extension requires a Show Command account. When you sign in, your email and password are sent over HTTPS directly to showcommand.live to authenticate you; the extension then stores only the returned access token locally (never your password). Your credentials are sent solely to Show Command's own authentication service.

What the extension does NOT do

  • It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.
  • It shows no advertising and performs no analytics, tracking, profiling, or fingerprinting.
  • It never sells or rents your data.
  • Aside from the sign-in step above, it never collects payment card numbers or other financial information, and it never stores your password.
  • It reads pages only on whatnot.com (the live-show chat) and otherwise contacts only showcommand.live to sign you in — it does not read any other websites you visit.

Permissions and why they are used

  • storage — save your request queue and settings locally in your browser.
  • sidePanel — show the request queue in Chrome's side panel.
  • tabs — locate your open Whatnot tab so the extension can read that show's chat and relay requests to it.
  • Access to whatnot.com — read the live-show chat on the page so requests can be detected.
  • Access to showcommand.live — sign you in to your Show Command account.

Data retention and your control

  • Data stored locally stays in your browser until you clear it or remove the extension.
  • Data you send to your own team server is retained and controlled entirely by you, under your own policies.

Children

The extension is a tool for sellers and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
